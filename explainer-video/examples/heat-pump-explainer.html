Moving heat, not making it
It’s 2 °C outside.
There’s still heat in that air.
Very cold refrigerant flows through the outdoor coil.
It evaporates, soaking up heat from the air.
An electric compressor squeezes the gas.
Squeezed tight, it gets hot enough to heat your home.
Indoors, the hot gas gives up its heat and condenses.
An expansion valve drops the pressure, and the loop repeats.
Often around 3 units of heat for every 1 of electricity.
Typically 2.5–4×, depending on the weather.
